Questions from Pune

Is AIS the same as BIS ISI?

No. AIS are Automotive Industry Standards used for vehicle and component approval, while the ISI Mark is BIS's product certification mark. A component may need one, the other or neither.

Do I need to be in Pune to work with Qualitance India?

No. Our office is in Noida, and BIS applications are filed online. We work with Pune manufacturers through video calls and handle documents remotely.

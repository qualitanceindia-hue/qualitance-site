Questions from Delhi

Do small units in Okhla or Mayapuri need BIS certification?

Only if the product is covered by a QCO, or if you choose voluntary certification. Unit size does not exempt a product, though some QCOs give small enterprises extra time. Check the notification for your product.

Do I need to be in Delhi to work with Qualitance India?

No. Our office is in Noida, and BIS applications are filed online. We work with Delhi manufacturers through video calls and handle documents remotely.

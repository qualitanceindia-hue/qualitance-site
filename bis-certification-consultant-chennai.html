Questions from Chennai

Do electronics assembled near Chennai need CRS registration?

Notified electronics and IT products do, and the registration is granted to the factory. Contract manufacturers and brand owners need aligned documents, including matching names and model numbers.

No. Our office is in Noida, and BIS applications are filed online. We work with Chennai manufacturers through video calls and handle documents remotely.

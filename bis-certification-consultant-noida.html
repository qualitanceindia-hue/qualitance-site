Questions from Noida

Do I need CRS registration for chargers and power banks made in Noida?

Power adapters and power banks appear in the CRS list under IS 13252 (Part 1). We confirm the current category and standard for your model before you send samples for testing.

Do I need to be in Noida to work with Qualitance India?

No. Our office is in Noida, and BIS applications are filed online. We work with Noida manufacturers through video calls and handle documents remotely.

    Common questions

    What is a Quality Control Order (QCO)?

    A QCO is a government notification under the BIS Act, 2016 that makes certification against a specific Indian Standard compulsory for listed products. Covered products generally cannot be manufactured, imported or sold in India without the standard mark.

    Do foreign manufacturers need BIS certification?

    Yes, if the product is covered by a QCO and exported to India. Overseas factories apply through the BIS Foreign Manufacturers Certification Scheme (FMCS).

    Is every product covered by a QCO?

    No. Only notified products are covered, and the list keeps changing: several orders have been added, deferred or withdrawn since late 2025. Check the current notification for your product.

    How long does BIS certification take?

    It depends on the product, the scheme, laboratory testing and factory inspection. We give a realistic timeline after reviewing your product and documents.
